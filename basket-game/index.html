Round 1 / 6

Task: Counting Items Sold
Checkers Sixty60 wants to find out which items are sold the most.
You are given a list of items that were bought by customers.

Your job is to:

  • Look at the list of items
  • Count how many times each item appears
  • Use tally marks to help you count
  • Write the total number in the frequency column

Goal: Complete the table to show how many times each item was sold.

Audio Guide:

Update your tally table dynamically as labeled items appear in each new basket layout.
